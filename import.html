Update the places

  1. On the Google account with the lists, open takeout.google.com ↗.
  2. Click “Deselect all”, tick Saved, and export.
  3. Drop the .zip (or the .csv files inside it) below. Nothing leaves your computer.

  1. In the GitHub repository for this site, open the data folder.
  2. Choose Add file › Upload files, drop the new places.json, and click Commit changes.
  3. The site updates within a minute or two.
